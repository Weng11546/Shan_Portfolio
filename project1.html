Project overview

ExpenseFlow is a mobile application designed to help users track their expenses and manage their finances effectively.

Problem statement

Many individuals struggle to keep track of their expenses, leading to financial mismanagement and difficulty in budgeting. ExpenseFlow aims to provide a simple and intuitive solution for users to monitor their spending habits.

Objectives

  • To develop a mobile application that helps users monitor and manage their daily expenses efficiently.
  • To allow users to easily record and categorize daily expenses in real-time.
  • To provide users with insightful reports and analytics about their spending patterns.

Target users

  • Employees

System architecture

The Expense Tracker mobile application follows a three-layer architecture consisting of the Mobile Frontend, Backend Server, and Database

System design

The design of the Expense Tracker mobile application focuses on simplicity and ease of use. The user interface is intuitive, allowing users to quickly navigate through the app and record their expenses with minimal effort. The app also provides visual representations of spending patterns through charts and graphs, making it easier for users to understand their financial habits.
